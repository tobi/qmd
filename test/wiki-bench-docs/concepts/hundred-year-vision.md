# Hundred Year Vision

## Definition

Build companies (and steward the planet) so they're not embarrassing in 100 years; the long-arc/time-horizon frame.

## Timeline

> How Tobi framed this across appearances (date → source → framing note).

- **2011-09-07** — [[sources/2011-business-of-software]] · `SRC-2011-09-07-001` — 2011 BoS talk: companies exist to delight customers, not chase money; uses DodoCase, the NUMMI/Toyota-vs-GM story, and Shopify's peer-recognition 'unicorn' bonus system (1% of revenue).
- **2020-06-15** — [[sources/2020-my-climate-journey]] · `SRC-2020-06-15-001` — 2020 climate interview: Shopify's $5M/yr Sustainability Fund, the first-order-from-Pennsylvania origin restated, learning climate as a non-expert leader, and the case that companies must price in their externalities.
- **2026-01-18** — [[sources/2026-founders-david-senra]] · `SRC-2026-01-18-001` — 2026 Founders podcast with David Senra: a biographical-historical framing of 21 years of Shopify, the founder-as-student-of-history thesis (Rockefeller, Edwin Land, Napoleon, Jobs), and the long arc.
