# Autonomy

## Definition

Tobi's case for autonomy rests on the NUMMI story: Toyota took over GM's failing plant with the same workers and gave them "an incredible amount of autonomy… every person had a rip cord with which [they] could stop the supply chain." The lesson he draws: "no one wanted to give a lot of autonomy to these dispensable people. However, as soon as someone did, they completely out competed everything." Autonomy is the design choice that turns "dispensable" workers into the people who catch problems — and (per the trust battery) it's what a charged battery buys you. ([[sources/2011-business-of-software]])
