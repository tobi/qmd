# The Motley Fool Interviews Shopify Co-Founder and CEO Tobi Lutke (2017)

> Raw packet: [[sources/2017-motley-fool-tom-gardner]]

## Summary

A 2017 investor-oriented interview (Shopify then a ~$10B company). Tobi frames Shopify's culture as deliberately *not* insulating people from outside change — instead valuing how fast the org adjusts. He leans on Taleb's *Antifragile*, describes hiring high-future-potential people (not industry insiders) in a Canadian [[concepts/secondary-markets|secondary market]], and uses a "pendulum" metaphor for management: teams swing past the right point and someone has to build a scaffold to hold the pendulum in the green zone.

## Key Points

- Culture is to be made better continuously, not frozen; "thriving on change" is the hardest value for new hires to absorb.
- Antifragility (Taleb): apparent "stability" isn't stable; you must train for disruption (e.g. closing offices for a month to learn remote work).
- Hiring: high future potential over industry expertise; "there isn't really a speed limit to personal growth."
- "Pendulum" management: every team oscillates around a target; the job is to build scaffolds that hold the pendulum in the green zone where it matters, and let it swing freely where experimentation is wanted.
- Refuses to invest in merchants — would make Shopify a kingmaker and compromise [[concepts/platform-neutrality|platform neutrality]].
- Going public was "a tactic, not a milestone"; walked away from higher private valuations.
