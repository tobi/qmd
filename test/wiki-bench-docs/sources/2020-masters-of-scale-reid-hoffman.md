# Be a platform, with Tobi Lutke (Masters of Scale, Reid Hoffman)

> Raw packet: [[sources/2020-masters-of-scale-reid-hoffman]]

## Summary

Reid Hoffman's *Masters of Scale* episode (Feb 2020) on the platform thesis. Tobi restates the Bill-Gates platform test and Shopify's invisible-by-design, merchant-aligned positioning — the counter-model to Amazon's consumer-facing aggregator. The episode is a canonical statement of [[concepts/platform-vs-aggregator|platform vs aggregator]] as a strategic polarity in commerce.

## Key Points

- Platform test: you are only a platform if the value of the ecosystem on top exceeds the value of the company that owns the platform.
- Shopify is deliberately invisible: it exists to make merchants look good on their own domains; the opposite of Amazon's consumer-facing aggregator model.
- Merchant-first neutrality: Shopify does not compete with its merchants and resists becoming a kingmaker.
- Scale context: ~$40B+ GMV, 800,000+ merchants (2019–20 era figures cited across sources).
