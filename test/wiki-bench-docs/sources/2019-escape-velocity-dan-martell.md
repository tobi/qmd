# Tobi Lütke The Creator of Shopify (Escape Velocity, Dan Martell)

> Raw packet: [[sources/2019-escape-velocity-dan-martell]]

## Summary

A 2019 founder-to-founder conversation with Dan Martell. Tobi states Shopify's purpose bluntly: "arm the rebels" — give many people the tools to compete with Amazon. The through-line is a single [[concepts/driving-question|driving question]] ("why is it so hard to build an internet company? what would the world look like if entrepreneurship were easy and common?") that he says has consumed him for 15 years and that Shopify is "a collaborative inquiry into." He argues the world is "loopy, not linear" (systems thinking from programming), that a company improves only when its people have eureka moments (hence a large Talent Acceleration/coaching team), and that in a 50%-growth company you must "requalify for your job every year." Includes the Harley Finkelstein recruitment origin.

## Key Points

- "Arm the rebels" — Shopify exists to let many people compete with Amazon; good for the internet.
- Becoming a billionaire changed nothing ("the clouds did not part"); money is just a form of energy, time is higher-leverage.
- Driving question (15 years): why is starting an internet business so hard? Shopify = the inquiry; "what sucks about starting online businesses? → that's the next thing to work on."
- Generalist pride: draws on many fields; never wanted to be "a mobile developer" — too confined.
- VC rejection story: ~2008 a VC passed saying the addressable market (40–50k stores) was too small — Tobi later: "you were right; Shopify was the solution to the problem you identified."
- Following the question defies industry "swim lanes" (shopping cart → multi-channel → logistics).
- "The world is loopy, not linear" — programmers know systems/reinforcing loops; company building is only now catching up.
- A company improves only when people have breakthroughs → large Talent Acceleration (50+); "you have to requalify for your job every year" in a fast-growing firm.
- Harley Finkelstein origin: met at an Ottawa entrepreneur meetup (~2007), Harley became a relentless squeaky-wheel merchant, Tobi made his account free, then recruited him — the first hire "completely different from me," broadening culture fit.
