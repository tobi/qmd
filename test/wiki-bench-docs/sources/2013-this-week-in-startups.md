# This Week in Startups - Tobi with Jason Calacanis (TWiST Ep. 369)

> Raw packet: [[sources/2013-this-week-in-startups]]

## Summary

Jason Calacanis interviews Tobi in Ottawa in 2013, when Shopify had ~60,000 merchants. The conversation covers the now-familiar Snowdevil origin (selling snowboards on Yahoo Stores, hating the tools, building on Ruby on Rails) and Tobi's retail thesis: that the future belongs to commerce in "the stuff that doesn't come with a barcode" — the long tail of curated, niche, maker goods — and that big-box physical retail (he predicts Best Buy's death) is being structurally disrupted.

## Key Points

- Shopify at ~60,000 customers; Ottawa-based "online store software as a service."
- Origin: tried Yahoo Stores for Snowdevil, found existing ecommerce tools unusable; programmer-by-trade Tobi wanted to start a retail business and ended up building the platform instead.
- Rails community lineage: references 37signals/Basecamp, DHH, the Chicago/Denmark Rails scene as formative.
- Retail thesis: the interesting growth is in non-barcode, curated, niche products (e.g. Etsy-style, Kickstarter, makers), not mass retail.
- Predicts Best Buy will die; physical big-box retail is structurally vulnerable.
- Recruiting hustle: a "stunt recruitment day" at IBM during Ottawa layoffs.
