# Leo Laporte Interviews Tobi Lutke

> Raw packet: [[sources/2006-leo-laporte]]

## Summary

A short TV segment from late 2006, only months after Shopify's June 2006 launch, in which Tobi demos the product on Leo Laporte's tech-help show. Tobi frames Shopify as software that accidentally outgrew its snowboard shop origin, and walks Leo through the theme engine (the custom template language that became [[concepts/liquid|Liquid]]), a deliberately short two-page checkout, and Shopify's crowdsourced translation system. The segment is a useful early snapshot of Tobi's user-friction thinking before Shopify was a known company.

## Key Points

- Shopify "was not supposed to become Shopify" — it began as in-house software for the Snowdevil snowboard store; the team later decided to turn the software into a product.
- The theme engine (a custom template language) is what lets every store look completely different, going beyond plain CSS/HTML; this predates and motivates [[concepts/liquid|Liquid]].
- Checkout is intentionally two pages; Shopify deliberately does **not** force buyers to create an account — "it's only good for the shop owner, not actually good for the user."
- Internationalization is crowdsourced: Shopify writes the English strings and lets any user add translations, an early example of [[concepts/default-to-open|default-to-open]] / community contribution.
- Tobi is introduced as a "core member of the Ruby On Rails development team," positioning Shopify's craft lineage early.
