# Shopify: Tobias Lütke (How I Built This, Guy Raz)

> Raw packet: [[sources/2019-how-i-built-this]]

## Summary

Guy Raz's biographical interview for NPR's *How I Built This* (Aug 2019). It opens with the Kylie Cosmetics proof-of-concept — a billion-dollar brand with seven employees running entirely on Shopify — and the scale stats ($40B+ GMV, 800,000+ merchants, market cap just below GM and Ford). It then traces Tobi's life: a childhood in Koblenz with undiagnosed ADHD and dyslexia, dropping out after 10th grade into a Siemens programming apprenticeship under a rebel mentor (Jürgen Starr), falling for snowboarding and meeting his wife Fiona in Whistler (~2000), moving to Ottawa in 2002, and the failed Dortmund browser startup that preceded Snowdevil.

## Key Points

- Kylie Cosmetics as the emblematic Shopify story: ~$1B company, 7 full-time employees, manufacturing outsourced, marketing = iPhone/Instagram, all sales & fulfillment on Shopify.
- 2019 scale: $40B+ GMV, 800,000+ merchants, market cap just below General Motors and Ford; yet low-profile ("invisible by design").
- Childhood: Koblenz, Germany; diagnosed with learning disabilities (likely ADHD), dyslexic; bored by the solution-first German curriculum — "if I don't understand the problem… it's hard to learn a solution."
- Left school after 10th grade for a programming apprenticeship at Siemens; found a "rebel" mentor, Jürgen Starr (long hair, BMW motorcycle, proto-skunkworks), and got paid to program from age ~17.
- Met Fiona snowboarding in Whistler ~2000; she came to Germany, then he followed her back to Ottawa in 2002 ("Canada is America for Europeans").
- First Canada-era work was remote for a Dortmund browser startup that died post-dotcom.
- Frugal-founder details: rode a used bike to VC pitches; lived in his in-laws' spare room even after Shopify had raised $100M+.
- CEO transition: "I'm not good… my team was very, very patient with me"; "humans are not like [computers]."
