# Building a Modern Business (Invest Like the Best #173)

> Raw packet: [[sources/2020-invest-like-the-best-173]]

## Summary

A 2020 Colossus interview coinciding with the launch of the consumer-facing **Shop** app (a merge of Arrive package-tracking + Shop Pay). Tobi frames merchant-obsession as a strategic focus choice (avoiding the zero-sum marketplace dynamic where "the merchants' margins are the opportunity and future fees of the marketplace"), explains why Shopify never built a marketplace (couldn't answer why buyers would prefer it to Google/Amazon), and explores focus as multi-dimensional, the German cultural "minimum quality bar," and video games (StarCraft/Factorio) as attention-training.

## Key Points

- Shop app launch: first consumer-facing Shopify brand; Arrive (16M installs) + Shop Pay merged; "a shop of shops" intensifying buyer-merchant relationships, not a marketplace.
- Merchant-obsession = focus of audience; predicting success more than almost any other choice; "we want to make other people look great."
- Marketplace rejected: a marketplace "derives power by diffusing the influence of any individual merchant" and pits them against each other — opposite of merchant-first. (2007 Shopify briefly *was* a marketplace.)
- Focus is multi-dimensional, not binary.
- German "minimum quality bar" — cultural, not engineering; high bar slows shipping (Germany/Austria/Switzerland less entrepreneurial); US lower bar ships faster; China lower still.
- Video games as deliberate practice for attention allocation (StarCraft, Factorio).
